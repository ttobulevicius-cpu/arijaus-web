Instead of giving your home address, choose your courier network (Omniva, InPost Paczkomat, DPD Pickup Locker, Packeta Z-Box, GLS) and paste the locker terminal address/code. When the courier loads your parcel into the locker, you receive an automated SMS PIN on your phone to open the locker door.
Because parcel locker terminals do not have couriers waiting for you. The locker door ONLY opens with the 6-digit PIN or QR code sent straight to your phone number and email address. Always double-check your phone number!
When you confirm your order, our website generates a unique Order ID and shows our European IBAN details. You transfer the funds via your banking app with that Order ID in the payment reference. Once confirmed, your order is dispatched immediately.
